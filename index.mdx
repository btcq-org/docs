**If you control Bitcoin in an eligible output, you can claim an equal amount of QBTC. QBTC is a separate chain secured by post-quantum signatures.**

A large quantum computer could break Bitcoin's signatures. QBTC is a separate chain whose validators sign with ML-DSA, a NIST post-quantum signature scheme. The chain tracks the Bitcoin UTXO set. Eligibility is live: whoever controls an eligible unspent output at the time of the claim can claim it. Your Bitcoin stays on Bitcoin.

The self-claim supports P2PKH (`1…`) and P2WPKH (`bc1q…`) outputs today. A new output needs 144 Bitcoin confirmations (about one day) before it can claim.

## The core idea, briefly

Quantum computers will eventually break the math that protects Bitcoin addresses. Every Bitcoin address whose public key has ever been revealed (which includes every address that has ever spent) becomes vulnerable that day.

QBTC is a separate chain whose validators sign with ML-DSA, a NIST-standardized post-quantum signature scheme (FIPS 204). The chain keeps a continuous mirror of the Bitcoin UTXO set. To claim, your wallet signs with your Bitcoin key, and a proving service turns that signature into a zero-knowledge proof of control. The claim does not publish your Bitcoin public key on chain.

Nothing happens to your Bitcoin. No bridge, no peg, no locked coins, no custodian.
